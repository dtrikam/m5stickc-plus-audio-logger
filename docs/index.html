🖼️ Visual Hardware, LCD Screen & Architecture Guides

Tap any diagram below to inspect it full-screen in high resolution.

1. M5StickC Plus Hardware & Button Guide

Front [M5] Button starts/stops recording. Side Button (BtnB) short-press emails your summary + full transcript immediately; holding for 1.5s toggles Online/Offline mode. USB-C Power keeps the screen at 100% brightness without dimming.

2. End-to-End System Architecture

Shows how 16 kHz PDM audio is compressed into 4-bit IMA ADPCM, streamed live via Dual-Core Ping-Pong buffering over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (or buffered into 2.62 MB internal SPIFFS flash when offline and auto-offloaded), transcribed by Gemini Flash, stored in SQLite, and emailed daily.

3. Live LCD Screen States & Status Bar Reference

Explains all 4 color-coded LCD screen cards (Green Standby, Red Live Cloud Recording, Amber Offline Flash VAD Recording, and Blue Instant Email Confirmation) along with the top-right USB 100% vs. battery indicator and bottom counters.
